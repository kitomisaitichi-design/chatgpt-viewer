Local semantic search

Optional multilingual embeddings find similar meaning without uploading chats. Select Semantic or Hybrid in search, or click below. Setup downloads the portable CPU engine and multilingual model automatically, then indexes your chats. No Python installation or restart is needed. The first setup needs internet and can take several minutes and over 1 GB of disk; searches then work offline.

Organization backup

Save categories, manual order, pins, colors, and reading positions separately from your original exports.

Export folders

Choose the folder containing your exports or place the viewer beside them. Your starting folder loads first. Discovery then expands through nearby folders up to the selected parent level, without waiting to find every file.

Export files stay untouched. Symlinks, system folders, dependency caches, and the viewer's index are skipped. A drive root is never automatically scanned.
