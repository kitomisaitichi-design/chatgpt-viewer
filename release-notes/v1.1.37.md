# Offline Chat Viewer 1.1.37

- Replace large lower sidebar buttons with one compact icon dock: Bookmarks, Trash, interface size, Settings, and More.
- Move scan/rescan, archive status, shortcuts and mark-matches-read into a small contextual popover above the dock. Closing it returns the space to the conversation list.
- Add live, persistent sidebar scale from 70% to 115%, including Auto, Small, Compact and Normal presets. Auto adapts to the window height.
- Compress bulk selection into small icon controls with an always-visible selected count; retain the independent draggable/resizable action panel and full selection behavior.
- Scale sidebar navigation, groups, chat rows and bottom controls together without changing the message reader's font size.
- Preserve the underlying bookmarks, trash, backup, selection, scan and settings actions and data.

The update changes layout only. Saved chats, folders, backups, preferences and connection permissions are not migrated or deleted.
