# Offline Chat Viewer 1.1.40

Refine the v1.1.39 conversation scrubber to match the compact iPhone-like visual reference:

- Remove the dark background capsule, permanent track, borders, shadows, and scaling effects from the scrubber itself.
- Keep 19 separate **1-pixel-thin, 12-pixel-long straight horizontal dashes** floating over the page background.
- Gently animate which lines are brightest as the actual conversation scroll position changes; dim idle dashes without creating a solid bar.
- Retain mouse-wheel scrolling, hover-only translucent previews, keyboard navigation, click/drag jumps into unloaded messages, and grouped commentary.
- Leave the regular scrollbar available on narrow displays.

No archived messages or preferences are modified.
