Document Find now advances through matches with Find/Enter and goes backward with Shift+Enter or the arrow controls. A polished panel below the toolbar shows the current match and total, case sensitivity, whole-word matching, regular expressions, and highlight-all. It stays open while interacting with the document; closing Find, closing the preview, or changing threads clears its session.

Matching runs in a cancellable worker with a timeout, a 500-character query limit, and a 10,000-match cap. Markdown/text search covers rendered preview text (first 1 MiB); PDF search covers extracted text across pages; Word search stays inside its sandbox; spreadsheet search covers the current worksheet preview. The active match is highlighted and scrolled into view. Invalid expressions and empty results are shown in the panel. Existing local-copy selection, document rendering and safe preview limits remain intact.

Keep .viewer-data and shared preferences when updating. Personal archives and fixtures are excluded from the release.
